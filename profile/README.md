# TradingLookup

We publish the numbers trading firms put out about themselves, cleaned into tables you can download, cite and check against the original.

| Series | What it records | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CFD loss-rate series | Share of retail accounts losing money, from each provider's own risk warning: 31 providers as of 6 Oct 2026 (provisional) | [CFD loss rates](https://tradinglookup.com/brokers/cfd-loss-rates/) |
| Crowd Gauge | Trader positioning across 20 markets, built from CFTC Commitments of Traders reports (as of 9 Oct 2026) | [Crowd Gauge](https://tradinglookup.com/data/crowd-gauge/) |
| Economic calendar | Release dates copied from official statistics schedules: 178 events as of 14 Oct 2026 | [Economic calendar](https://tradinglookup.com/economic-calendar/) |

**Download the files:** [GitHub (CSV + schema)](https://github.com/tradinglookup/data) · [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tradinglookup/cfd-retail-loss-rates-by-provider-2018-2026) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TradingLookup/cfd-loss-rates)
